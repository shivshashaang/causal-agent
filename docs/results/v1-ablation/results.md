# Benchmark v1: no-tools ablation without the correlation table

Generated by `python -m causal_agent.eval.run` from 30 datasets. Every number below comes from that run.

`LLM, no tools, no correlations (ablation)` is the no-tools baseline with the correlation table removed from its summary and nothing else changed. It is reported in addition to `LLM, no tools`, not in place of it.

An estimate is counted as correct when it is within 0.10 standard deviations of the outcome of the true ATE. Errors marked (SD) are in those units.

## Validity, kept separate from accuracy

A method is valid when its adjustment set passes the backdoor criterion, overlap is adequate, and its model assumption is not known to fail. For the doubly robust estimator the model-fit assumption cannot be read from the ground truth, so valid means valid as far as the truth can show. The no-tools LLM's method is self-reported.

| Approach | Adjustment set valid | Estimator valid | Valid and accurate | Valid, inaccurate | Invalid, accurate | Invalid and inaccurate | No recognized estimator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LLM, no tools | 30/30 | 24/30 | 24 | 0 | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| LLM, no tools, no correlations (ablation) | 30/30 | 24/30 | 14 | 10 | 4 | 2 | 0 |

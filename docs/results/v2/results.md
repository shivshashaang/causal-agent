## Estimator-disagreement check

Run by the evaluation on each final method. It compares the method's estimate with one from the other model family on the same adjustment set. Disagreement means the two estimators cannot both be right; it is not proof that the reported estimate is biased. Fractions are out of the inaccurate or the accurate estimates checked. Inconclusive results are counted on their own. For a no-tools approach the check runs the method the model named, not the number it reported.
